Refund & Refill Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

TermsPrivacyRefund & Refill
Quick summary: you can cancel a wallet-paid order yourself before it starts. Once it has started, you can send a cancellation request, and the admin reviews it and decides. Refill applies only to Completed orders of refill-enabled services.

1. Order cancellation

  • An order paid from your wallet and still Pending can be cancelled directly from "Order History". The money returns to your wallet immediately.
  • For Processing orders, or orders paid via bKash/Nagad/Binance, you need to send a "Cancel request". The admin reviews it and lets you know.
  • An order that has already started with the provider may not be cancellable.

2. When you get a refund

  • If a wallet-paid order is Rejected/Canceled, the money is returned to your wallet automatically.
  • If an order paid by another method is Rejected, contact support; after verification the amount is added to your wallet or returned another way.
  • If the service cannot be delivered (for example, it has been discontinued), the amount for the undelivered part is refundable.

3. When you do not get a refund

  • If you gave a wrong link, wrong username or wrong quantity.
  • If the account or post is private, or you change/delete the link while the order is running.
  • For a completed order, because you changed your mind afterwards.
  • For some services, if the count drops because of the platform (see Refill below).

4. Refill

  • Refill applies only to Completed orders of services with the Refill tag. If there is no tag, there is no refill.
  • You can request a refill within 30 days of completion (this may vary by service).
  • Request a refill yourself with the Refill button in Order History. After each request you must wait 24 hours, and an order allows at most 5 refills.
  • Refill cannot be given if you make the account private, change or remove the username/link.
  • Check the Refill tag on the service card to see whether refill is available.

5. How long it takes

Wallet refunds are instant. Refunds by other methods and refill requests are usually reviewed within 24 hours; it may take a little longer at busy times.

6. Contact

Tell us via WhatsApp or a support ticket, and include your order ID.
