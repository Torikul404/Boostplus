Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

TermsPrivacyRefund & Refill

1. What information we collect

  • Mobile number — to identify your wallet account and orders
  • Order details — link/username, service, quantity, price, time
  • Payment details — payment method, TrxID, and a payment proof screenshot if you upload one. We do not collect card or bank account numbers.
  • Support tickets — your messages and attached files
  • Data stored in your browser — login session token (up to 30 days), theme preference and some site cache. These stay on your device.

2. How we use it

  • To process orders, verify payments, handle refunds/refills and provide support
  • To prevent fraud and abuse
  • To improve the site's performance and our services

3. Who it is shared with

  • Service providers — only the link and quantity are sent to fulfil an order. Your mobile number or PIN is never sent.
  • Admin notifications — order and payment updates go to our admin channel (Telegram).
  • Infrastructure — data is kept in Google Sheets/Drive and the site runs on Cloudflare. These providers may keep standard server logs under their own terms.
  • We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties unless legally required.

4. PIN and security

Your PIN is not stored directly; it is kept in encrypted (hashed) form. Repeated wrong PIN attempts temporarily block login. Still, no online system is 100% secure, so do not share your PIN with anyone.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising. Your browser's local storage is used to remember your session and theme; clearing your browser data will remove them.

6. Your rights

You can ask to view, correct or delete your information. However, we may need to keep transaction records for a period for accounting and dispute resolution. Send your request via WhatsApp or a support ticket.

7. Children's information

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
