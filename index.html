Order Rules

Take a minute to read these rules before ordering — most problems can be avoided here.

Before you order
  1. Check the link and quantity. No refund for a wrong link or wrong quantity.
  2. Do not place multiple orders on the same link at once. Place a new order after the previous one is Completed.
  3. Do not make the profile/post Private or delete it while the order is running. The link must stay public.
  4. Refill only applies to services tagged Refill / Guarantee. Even if a service says "Non-Drop" or "HQ", there is no refill after a drop unless Refill is listed separately.
  5. Once you accept a Partial refund, you cannot claim a refill/refund on that order later.
  6. An order that has already started may not be cancellable. Open a ticket for cancellation or Partial — we will check and let you know.
  7. Orders may start late during busy periods. Check the status in Track Order first, then open a ticket if needed.
  8. Enter the correct Transaction ID after payment. A wrong or previously used TrxID will hold the order in verification.
  9. Never share your password. We only need a public link.
  10. Services must not be used for illegal or unethical purposes. Such orders may be cancelled.

Refill and cancellation rules
  • Refill is only for Completed orders of services tagged Refill. Request it within 30 days of completion (may vary by service).
  • After one refill request, wait 24 hours; up to 5 times per order.
  • A Pending order paid from the wallet can be cancelled directly from Order History. For a Processing order, send a Cancel request.
  • No refund if the link, username or quantity is wrong, or if the account or post is private.
How long does it take
  • Wallet refunds are instant.
  • Refunds to other methods and refill requests are usually reviewed within 24 hours; it may take a little longer in busy periods.
